An SMS right after another is read within seconds.
* Fixed: when two SMS came seconds apart (often a bank SMS and a UPI SMS for one payment), the second one could show up only later, at the next SMS or when the app next started. It is now read within about 10 seconds, with its "Payment detected" notification
* Fixed: a bank SMS whose sender comes without the hyphen (VMHDFCBK) is now known by its bank
