Expenses and splits, added for you

SumBuddy takes the typing out of tracking your month and splitting with friends. It picks up payments from your bank and UPI SMS, so you only choose to add, split or ignore each one.

Download the APK See the source on GitHub

Version 0.3.2 · Made for India: rupees, Indian banks and UPI · Not on Google Play

How it works

Your payments, picked up for you

  1. 1. Pay as usual

    Your bank and UPI apps send their usual payment SMS.

  2. 2. SumBuddy finds the payment

    It reads those SMS on your phone and picks out the amount and where you paid. The SMS never leave the phone.

  3. 3. Add, split or ignore

    One tap adds it to your month or splits it with friends. Nothing is added without you.

What it does

What SumBuddy does

  • Spending by category

    This month against last month, added up for you.

  • You decide each payment

    Payments are picked up automatically. Add, split or ignore each one, with Add all and Undo.

  • Card bills

    What is due, what friends owe you of it, and a reminder 3 days before.

  • Split with friends

    Groups, who owes whom, settle up by UPI, cash or bank transfer, and a WhatsApp link for friends without the app.

  • Family month

    Share one monthly total with your partner, with no owing between you.

  • Import from Splitwise

    Bring a group across from a Splitwise export file.

Splitting, Family month and Import from Splitwise need a Google sign-in. Tracking your month needs none.

Privacy

Private by default

Payments are found on your phone, and the ones you don’t split stay there. No ads and no analytics. Signing in with Google is optional, only for splitting with friends, Family month and Import from Splitwise. Without it, nothing about you is stored on the server. Push messages for groups use Google’s Firebase Cloud Messaging, only after you sign in.

Read the privacy policy

Stays on your phone

  • Bank and UPI SMSRead on the phone to find payments. Never uploaded, never in logs.
  • Payments you don’t splitWith their accounts, cards, card bills and notes.

Sent to the server only after you sign in

  • An expense you split or mark FamilyOnly that expense: description, category, amount, date, who paid and the shares.
  • Your name, phone number and UPI IDYour name from Google, and the phone number and UPI ID you add. Shown to people in your groups; your name and UPI ID also appear on a payment link you send.
